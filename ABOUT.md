# About

This study acts as an extension to the study "Analyzing the Impact of Frametime Spikes on Navigation-Based Tasks in 2D Platformers."

Players will play through a range of tasks (e.g. jumping over a gap). Frametime spikes are triggered at critical points in the task to assess the impact of frametime spikes on that particular task.

After each checkpoint, players are asked to rank the visual smoothness of that checkpoint. This allows us to assess the impace of frametime spikes on players' quality of experience.
